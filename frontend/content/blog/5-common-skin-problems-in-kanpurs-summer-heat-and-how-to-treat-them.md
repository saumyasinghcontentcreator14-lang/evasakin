Kanpur ki tez garmi, dhoop aur dust pollution seedha aapki skin par asar dalte hain. Yahan hum 5 sabse aam summer skin problems aur unke solutions bata rahe hain.



\## 1. Suntan aur Pigmentation



Roz dhoop mein nikalne se tanning aur dark patches ho sakte hain. Ghar se nikalne se 20 minute pehle SPF 30+ sunscreen zaroor lagayein.



\## 2. Acne aur Breakouts



Pasine aur oil ki wajah se pores band ho jate hain, jisse pimples badh jate hain. Din mein do baar gentle face wash use karein.



\## 3. Skin Rashes aur Irritation



Zyada garmi mein heat rashes common hain. Loose, cotton clothes pehnein aur skin ko dry rakhein.



\## 4. Dehydrated Skin



Garmi mein sweat se skin apna moisture kho deti hai. Din mein 8-10 glass paani piyein aur lightweight moisturizer use karein.



\## 5. Fungal Infections



Humidity fungal infections ka risk badha deti hai, khaas kar folds wali jagah par. Skin ko dry aur clean rakhna zaroori hai.



Agar in problems mein se koi bhi persistent ho ya bigadti ja rahi ho, Eva Skin Clinic mein humse consultation lein — hum aapki skin type ke hisaab se sahi treatment suggest karenge.
