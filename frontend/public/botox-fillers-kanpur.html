Understanding the Treatment
Botox & Fillers — What's
the Difference?
💉

Botox (Botulinum Toxin)

Botox is a purified protein that temporarily relaxes the facial muscles responsible for expression lines — like forehead wrinkles, crow's feet, frown lines, and bunny lines. It does not add volume; it softens movement-caused wrinkles.

✓ Forehead lines
✓ Crow's feet
✓ Frown lines
✓ Lip flip
✓ Jaw slimming
Results: 3–6 months  | 
🌸

Dermal Fillers (Hyaluronic Acid)

Fillers are injectable gels that restore lost facial volume, define contours, and plump areas that have become hollow with age. They can enhance lips, cheeks, jawline, chin, and under-eye hollows — naturally and safely.

✓ Lip enhancement
✓ Cheek volume
✓ Under-eye (tear trough)
✓ Jawline definition
✓ Chin augmentation
Results: 6–18 months  |

FAQs
Common Questions About
Botox & Fillers in Kanpur
Is Botox safe in Kanpur? +
Yes, when performed by a qualified dermatologist using genuine FDA-approved products. At Eva Skin Clinic, all Botox treatments are administered by Dr. Sandeep Uttam (dermatology) using certified, authentic products in a sterile environment.
How long do Botox results last? +
Botox results typically last 3–6 months depending on the area treated and individual muscle activity. Regular treatments may extend the duration over time as muscles gradually relax.
How long do dermal fillers last? +
Depending on the filler type and area, results can last 6–18 months. Lip fillers typically last 6–12 months; cheek and jawline fillers can last up to 18 months.
Does Botox hurt? +
Most patients describe it as a minor pinch. We apply a topical numbing cream before the procedure to ensure comfort. The entire treatment takes 20–30 minutes.
Can I combine Botox and fillers in the same session? +
Yes. Combination treatments are very common and often deliver the best results. For example, Botox for forehead lines combined with cheek or lip fillers for overall facial rejuvenation. Dr. Sandeep will guide you through the right combination during consultation.
