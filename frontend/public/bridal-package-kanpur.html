Why Plan Ahead
Why Bridal Skin Treatment
Must Start Early
Most brides make the mistake of starting skin treatment 2–4 weeks before the wedding. Real, lasting transformation requires 3–6 months.
📅

Deep Results Take Time

Laser treatments, PRP, and skin-lightening protocols require multiple sessions spaced weeks apart for visible results.

🧴

Skin Needs to Settle

After procedures like peels or laser, skin needs recovery time. Starting early means your skin is fully settled and glowing by the wedding.

💇

Hair Takes 3+ Months

PRP hair therapy and hair growth treatments show significant results only after 3–4 months of consistent sessions.

🧘

No Last-Minute Stress

A planned programme means zero skin emergencies a week before the wedding. You'll be completely stress-free.

FAQs
Bridal Package FAQs
When should I start my bridal skin treatment in Kanpur? +
Ideally 4–6 months before your wedding. This gives enough time for laser treatments, PRP hair therapy, and pigmentation protocols to show full results with adequate recovery time. Even starting 2–3 months before can show significant improvement with our intensive programmes.
Can the bridal package be customised? +
Yes, every bridal package at Eva Skin Clinic is customised to your skin type, specific concerns, wedding date, and budget. The packages listed are templates — Dr. Sandeep will personalise your exact plan during the free consultation.
Are bridal packages available for the groom too? +
Absolutely. We offer groom packages as well — focused on acne, tan removal, laser beard shaping, skin brightening, and hair fall treatment. Many couples book together for a joint consultation.
Will treatments cause any downtime before the wedding? +
We carefully plan the treatment timeline so that any procedures with brief recovery (like laser or medium peels) are done well in advance. The final week before the wedding involves only gentle, glow-enhancing treatments with zero downtime.
