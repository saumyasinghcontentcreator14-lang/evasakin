Kitna Hair Fall Normal Hai? — Kanpur Mein

Rozana 50–100 baal girna bilkul normal hai — yeh hair growth cycle ka natural part hai. Lekin agar isse zyada ho raha hai, baal patale lag rahe hain, hairline peeche ja rahi hai, ya jagah jagah baal kam ho rahe hain — yeh medical attention ka clear signal hai.

Kai log mahino tak ghar ke nuskhe aur shampoo badalne mein time waste karte hain. Jitni jaldi treatment shuru hogi, utne achhe results milenge — kyunki hair follicles ek baar permanently damage ho jaayein toh treatment kam effective hoti hai.

⚠️ Kanpur Specific Fact: Hard water, extreme heat aur high pollution level — Kanpur ki lifestyle mein hair fall ke multiple triggers hote hain jo dusre cities se alag hain. Generic treatment yahan kam kaam karti hai. Personalized diagnosis zaroori hai.

Frequently Asked Questions — Hair Fall Treatment Kanpur

Kanpur mein hair fall treatment ka kharcha kitna hai?

PRP se naye baal ugenge kya?
PRP dormant follicles ko wake up karta hai. Agar follicle permanently damage nahi hua toh new hair growth possible hai. 3–4 sessions ke baad results dikhne lagte hain. Early treatment mein sabse achhe results milte hain — isliye delay mat karo.
Kya female patients ke liye alag treatment hoti hai?
Haan. Women mein hair loss mostly hormonal causes se hoti hai — PCOS, thyroid, postpartum. Treatment plan men se alag hota hai. Minoxidil ki concentration bhi different hoti hai. Eva Skin mein women ke liye specialized consultation available hai.
Kanpur ke hard water se balon ko kaise bachayein?
Water softener filter lagaana, sulfate-free aur paraben-free shampoo use karna, aur regular scalp oil massage (coconut ya argan oil) helpful hai. Dr. Sandeep Uttam aapke scalp type ke hisaab se specific products recommend karte hain consultation mein.
COVID ke baad baal jhadna kab band hoga?
Post-COVID Telogen Effluvium mein generally 3–6 mahine mein hair fall slow hota hai naturally. Lekin nutritional support aur targeted treatment se recovery zyada tez hoti hai. Agar 6 mahine se zyada ho gaya hai toh consultation zaroor lein.
GFC aur PRP mein kya fark hai?
GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) PRP ka advanced version hai. PRP mein growth factors plasma ke saath hote hain, GFC mein sirf pure concentrated growth factors hote hain — zyada potent aur effective. Dr. Sandeep Uttam aapki condition dekhkar recommend karte hain ki kaun sa better rahega.
