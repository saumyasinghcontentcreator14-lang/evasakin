Yeh sirf "oily skin" ya "junk food" ka mamla nahi hai. Kanpur ke teen specific factors — pollution, hard water aur extreme heat — aapki skin par directly asar daalte hain aur ek "Kanpur-specific acne pattern" banate hain jo metro cities se thoda different hota hai.

Isi wajah se generic, internet pe mile hue solutions yahan utne effective nahi hote jitna ek personalized, local diagnosis hota hai.

Fungal Acne (Folliculitis)

Frequently Asked Questions — Acne Treatment Kanpur

Acne treatment Kanpur mein kitna kharcha hai?

Kanpur ka pollution aur hard water acne ko kaise affect karte hain?
Kanpur ki hard water skin ke natural oil barrier ko weaken karti hai, jisse pores asaani se block hote hain. High particulate pollution oil ke saath mil kar pores mein settle ho jaata hai — yeh combination breakouts ko trigger aur worsen, dono karta hai.
Acne scars ka treatment active acne se alag hota hai kya?
Haan. Active acne ka treatment oil control aur inflammation reduce karne par focus karta hai, jabki scars (boxcar, icepick, rolling) ke liye microneedling, subcision ya laser resurfacing jaisi alag procedures use ki jaati hain.
Hormonal acne ko kaise pehchanein?
Hormonal acne typically jawline aur chin ke around concentrate hota hai, monthly cycle ke saath pattern follow karta hai, aur OTC products se easily improve nahi hota. Adult women mein yeh kaafi common hai aur PCOS se bhi linked ho sakta hai.
Kya chemical peel Indian skin ke liye safe hai?
Haan, jab ek qualified dermatologist ke supervision mein sahi acid concentration aur skin-type-appropriate protocol se ki jaaye, toh chemical peels Indian skin tones ke liye generally safe aur effective hote hain.
Acne treatment mein results dikhne mein kitna time lagta hai?
Zyada-tar patients ko 4–8 hafton mein visible improvement dikhti hai, lekin full results — khaaskar severe ya cystic cases mein — 3–4 mahine tak lag sakte hain, consistent treatment ke saath.
