Warning Signs Jo Ignore Nahi Karni Chahiye

Acne jo 3 hafte se zyada persist kare

Hormonal ya bacterial acne ghar ke treatment se theek nahi hota. Khud se treatment par worse ho sakta hai aur permanent scars chhor sakta hai.

Sudden hair fall (100+ strands/day)

Iron deficiency, thyroid issue, ya PCOS ka direct sign. Blood test ke bina koi bhi hair treatment 50% effective hogi.

⚠
Dark patches jo badh rahi hain

Melasma, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, ya rare cases mein skin cancer — early diagnosis crucial. Kanpur ki tez dhoop mein sun damage fast hoti hai.

Rashes jo baar baar wapas aati hain

Eczema, psoriasis, ya contact dermatitis — proper diagnosis ke bina treatment kaam nahi karega.
