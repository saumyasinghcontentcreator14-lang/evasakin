Kanpur Mein Skin Doctor Se Kab Milein?

✦ Sahi Doctor Dhundho

Ek Achhe Skin Doctor Ki 5 Qualities

  1. Degree Verify Karein
    Dermatologist ke paas MBBS + MD (Dermatology) ya DVD hona chahiye. Certificate dikhaane mein koi accha doctor hichkichata nahi.
  2. Google Reviews Padhein
    Sirf star rating mat dekho — real patient reviews padho aur patterns dhundho.
  3. Diagnosis Pehle, Treatment Baad Mein
    Proper examination ke bina medicines likhne wale doctors avoid karein — yeh shortcut aapko nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai.
  4. Clear Pricing
    Treatment ka cost pehle se clear hona chahiye. Hidden charges wale clinics se door rahein.
  5. Follow-up Protocol
    Skin treatment ek visit mein nahi hoti. Jo doctor follow-up nahi karta, woh result bhi nahi deta.
