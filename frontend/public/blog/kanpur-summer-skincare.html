Important Baat

Expert Tips

Dr. Sandeep Uttam ke 6 Summer Skin Rules ☀️

Eva Skin mein rozana itne patients aate hain jo summer mein skin neglect karne ki wajah se problems face karte hain. Yeh 6 habits apnao:

  • 1
    SPF 50+ — Hero of the Season Ghar ke andar ho ya bahar — SPF lagana band mat karo. Har 2 ghante mein reapply zaroor karein. Indoor UV bhi real hoti hai.
  • 2
    Lightweight Moisturizer Switch Karo Thick cream summer mein enemy hai. Gel-based ya water-based moisturizer use karo. Skin ko breathe karne do.
  • 3
    Double Cleansing — Evening Mein Din bhar ki dust, sweat aur sunscreen — sab hata ke soye. Micellar water + gentle face wash ka combination best hai.
  • 4
    Pani Pani aur Sirf Pani Kanpur ki garmi mein minimum 3 litre paani daily. Electrolyte drinks bhi chalenge — skin hydration andar se aata hai.
  • 5
    Hands Off Policy for Pimples Sweat mein haath laga ke pimple dabana sabse common mistake hai. Bacteria spread hoti hai — aur scars bhi mushkil ho jaate hain.
  • 6
    Clinic Visit: Jab Ghar ka Nuskha Na Kaam Kare 2 hafte se zyada problem hai? Pigmentation bad ho rahi hai? Yeh signal hai ki professional help ki zaroorat hai — delay mat karo.
